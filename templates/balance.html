What these words mean

Cash available
Money you can spend right now. Buying shares reduces it; selling adds to it.
Value of investments
What your shares would be worth if you sold them all at today's prices. This moves every time the market does.
Total account value
Cash plus investments. This is the honest measure of how you're doing — a big cash balance isn't progress if your investments fell to pay for it.
