01 — The idea

Most people learn about investing by losing money slowly

The habits are what cost you: buying something you don't understand, selling the moment it dips, checking the price six times before lunch. You can practise all of them here for nothing, and notice how each one feels before it has a price.

02 — How it works

Three steps, no jargon

Step one

Find a company

Type a ticker — AAPL for Apple, MSFT for Microsoft. The name and today's price appear as you type, so a typo is obvious immediately.

Step two

Decide an amount

Buy a whole share or part of one. You always see the total cost and what your cash will be afterwards, before anything is confirmed.

Step three

Wait, and watch

Prices move. Your ledger shows what you paid, what it's worth now, and the difference — stated plainly, in either direction.
