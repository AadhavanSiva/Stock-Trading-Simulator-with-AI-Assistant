How it works

Three steps, no jargon

1

Pick a company

Type a ticker — AAPL for Apple, MSFT for Microsoft. You'll see the company's name and what one share costs right now.

2

Buy some shares

Decide how much to spend. You can buy part of a share, and you'll always see the total before anything is confirmed.

3

Watch what happens

Prices move. Your portfolio shows what you paid, what it's worth today, and whether you're up or down.

Why bother

The habits matter more than the returns

Most people learn about investing by losing money slowly. A practice portfolio lets you make the same mistakes for free — buying something you don't understand, selling in a panic, checking the price six times a day — and notice how each one feels before it costs you anything.
