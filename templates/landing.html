Why practise

Most people learn about investing by losing money slowly

The habits are what cost you: buying something you don't understand, selling the moment it dips, checking the price six times before lunch. You can practise all of them here for nothing, and notice how each one feels before it has a price.

How it works

Three steps, no jargon

Step 1

Find a company

Type a ticker, like AAPL for Apple or MSFT for Microsoft. The name and today's price appear as you type, so a typo is obvious at once.

Step 2

Decide an amount

Buy a whole share or part of one. You see the total cost and what your cash will be afterwards before anything is confirmed.

Step 3

Wait, and watch

Prices move. Your portfolio shows what you paid, what it's worth now, and the difference, stated plainly in either direction.
