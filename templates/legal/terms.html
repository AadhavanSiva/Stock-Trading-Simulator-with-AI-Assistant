What this is

Portfolio Tracker is a free stock market simulator and a personal learning project. It lets you practise buying and selling shares with money that does not exist, using delayed market prices. It is not a broker, not a financial institution, and not a service you can hold real value with. See the disclaimer for the detail.

Using it

You need a Google account to sign in. You are responsible for keeping that account secure; anyone who can sign in as you can see and change your simulated portfolio.

One account per person. Please do not:

  • create extra accounts to game the leaderboard, or choose a leaderboard name that impersonates someone else, is abusive, or is somebody's real name or email address;
  • automate requests, scrape the site, or make enough of them to affect its availability or its market-data provider's;
  • attempt to reach another user's data, or to bypass the limits and checks in the application;
  • use it for anything unlawful.

A leaderboard name that breaks these rules may be removed, and an account being used to attack the service may be suspended or deleted.
