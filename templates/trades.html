Realized
What a sale actually earned: the price you sold at, less what those shares had cost you on average. A gain only becomes real money once you sell, which is why a buy has no figure here.
Opening position
A holding you already had before this log existed. The shares and average cost are real; the date is the day your account was opened, because the original purchase was never recorded.
